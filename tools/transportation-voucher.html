🗺 Trip Details

Each trip generates one voucher (two vouchers per A4 page, stacked top & bottom). VESSEL NAME and PORT are filled from your saved settings. FROM / TO are picked from the saved locations of the currently selected port (Settings → Port) — switch the port and the dropdowns change accordingly. PASSENGERS' NAMES can be selected per trip (default: all selected) — only the checked passengers appear on that trip's voucher.
