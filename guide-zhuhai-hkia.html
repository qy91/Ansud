Traffic Guide

Zhuhai Port — Guidance for Passengers Travelling to Hong Kong International Airport (HKIA) by Bonded Bus

1 Arrive at Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge Zhuhai Port.

2 Go to the 3rd floor, find Line No. 11 (must be Line No. 11), show your QR code (provided by the boarding agent) to print out the bus ticket voucher, then proceed to conduct Immigration formalities.

3 Go to the 1st floor hall to obtain the bonded-bus ticket with the ticket voucher & the QR code (you may help yourself at the ticket machines or ask a staff member for help), then find the HK Airport Check-in Service for pre-check-in and luggage check-in.

Free Luggage Allowance

Each passenger can check in one piece of luggage for free, weighing no more than 20 kilograms, with total linear dimension no more than 158 cm (length + width + height). For overweight luggage, you will be charged RMB 80/20 kgs.

4 Go to the Bonded Bus waiting area, find boarding point A for ticket checking & boarding.
